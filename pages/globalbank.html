Facing mounting customer demand for faster financial services, GlobalBank turned to Source to modernize its lending operations. The result: a unified, AI-powered system that cuts decision times from days to hours while strengthening compliance and customer trust.

Introduction

GlobalBank has long been recognized for its reliability — but as fintech competitors accelerated, its traditional processes couldn’t keep up with customer expectations. Loan applications often required manual document checks and multi-department handoffs, delaying approvals and frustrating both teams and clients.

The leadership knew automation was the way forward, but the challenge lay in modernizing without disrupting sensitive regulatory workflows. That’s where Source came in.

Challenge

Loan approvals took an average of 4–5 business days, primarily due to manual document verification and fragmented systems. Risk analysts spent hours transferring data between platforms, while compliance teams faced recurring errors from inconsistent reporting.

GlobalBank needed to:

  • Cut down the time-to-approval without risking compliance

  • Reduce manual intervention across validation steps

  • Gain real-time visibility into every stage of the lending pipeline

The problem wasn’t effort — it was inefficiency. Every process worked, just not together.

Solution

Source introduced a connected, automation-first system built on AI document parsing, real-time data validation, and integrated risk scoring. Rather than replacing tools, the new framework linked them — bridging legacy systems with a lightweight automation layer.

Key innovations included:

  • AI-driven document processing to classify and extract critical data automatically

  • Dynamic risk scoring models trained on historical decision outcomes

  • End-to-end orchestration dashboards for full visibility across teams

  • Audit-ready workflows ensuring compliance with zero extra overhead

By combining automation with adaptive logic, Source transformed how GlobalBank moved information — not just faster, but smarter.

Result

Within six months, GlobalBank had achieved measurable transformation:

  • Loan decisions delivered 70% faster, now often within the same day

  • Manual review steps reduced by over 45%

  • Compliance accuracy reached 99.2%, with automatic reporting

  • Employee satisfaction improved as repetitive tasks disappeared

What began as an operational upgrade quickly evolved into a cultural shift — teams began to see automation not as a replacement, but as a quiet partner that made every process feel lighter and more precise.
